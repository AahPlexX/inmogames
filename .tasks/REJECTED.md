# Rejected

- Online leaderboards and server-authoritative multiplayer remain outside the current static-client/Firebase account-save platform unless a later explicit repository decision designs and approves them.
- Analytics, telemetry and ads remain outside the approved runtime boundary.

Optional user accounts, email/password sign-in and account-bound cloud saves are **not** rejected. Decision D-010 superseded the repository's original no-auth/no-database restriction on 2026-10-04; Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are the approved shared account/save platform.
