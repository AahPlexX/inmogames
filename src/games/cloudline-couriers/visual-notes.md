# Cloudline visual production direction

Cloudline uses an illustrated sky-courier language: warm brass, cream canvas, cool atmospheric blues, deep navigation ink, hand-built industrial landmarks, and high-contrast hazard weather. The shipped visual family must read as one world rather than a collection of generic icons.

## Motion hierarchy

- Ambient: subtle cloud/airship drift only.
- Turn anticipation: fly control compresses/charges before travel.
- Travel: courier lifts and settles at the destination.
- Hazard: storm/weather gets stronger impact motion than routine landings.
- Reward: credits/cargo use brief confirmation motion.
- Upgrade: landmark art changes state and receives a stronger transformation treatment.
- District/region milestones: highest celebration tier, still skippable and reduced-motion safe.

## Production rule

The current vector family is the first cohesive in-engine art tranche, not the final CLC-007 quality sign-off. CLC-007 remains open until rendered review confirms production quality across actual game scale and later campaign content supplies stateful regional/crew/weather/VFX assets. Emoji, crude primitive stand-ins, and generic placeholder icons are not acceptable release assets.
