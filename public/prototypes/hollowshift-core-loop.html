Instability forecast

How to play

  • Select a tile. Connected adjacent tiles can be entered with Move here.
  • Rotate or shift to rebuild routes. Lane shifts always show a preview before confirmation.
  • Survey reveals samples within one connected step of your position.
  • The Crawler advances after each committed action when a connected route exists.
